# Spatial Statistics for Decision-Making: A Blackfly Tale

Future of Parasitology, 13 October 2026. Claudio Fronterre, University of Birmingham.
25 minutes speaking plus 5 minutes questions. Storyboard v1, 11 October 2026.

## Agreed framing

Begin with the onchocerciasis elimination decision and the practical challenge of obtaining 6,000 blackflies for assessment. In the Nigerian work described by Claudio, states are the operational assessment units. Acknowledge that defining transmission zones remains an active scientific and programme question. Do not imply that administrative boundaries necessarily describe biological transmission boundaries.

Blackfly Finder predicts collection productivity and supports site allocation. It does not directly estimate infectivity or transmission risk. Explain its contribution to obtaining evidence for elimination assessment. Geographic representation matters alongside the total collected. An increase in expected catches alone does not establish representativeness or validate a stopping decision.

The main narrative follows decisions: why evidence is difficult to obtain, where to collect, how to allocate field resources, when to reconsider an unproductive site, and how future infectivity and epidemiological data could strengthen assessment.

## Statistics throughout the story

- Slide 4: describe variability and concentration in observed data.
- Slide 5: identify why the spatial distribution of evidence matters.
- Slide 7: separate the probability of a positive collection from positive catch magnitude.
- Slides 8–9: prediction, uncertainty and validation at unsampled locations.
- Slides 10–11: combine predictions with feasibility and geographic design constraints.
- Slides 13–15: update expectations after observing zeros and account for seasonality.
- Slide 16: assess performance against operational outcomes rather than expected catch alone.
- Slide 17: define what additional information is required for transmission inference.

Avoid a separate generic explanation of why statistics matters. Make each contribution apparent through the decision it supports. Do not promise mathematical optimality without a defined objective and supporting evaluation.

## Design direction

Use the supplied meeting_2026-10-08 Quarto theme as the base: 1280 × 720, Manrope, serif headings, restrained gold accent and original Birmingham logo. It is a personalised Birmingham-branded design, not an officially approved University template.

Increase figure area, reduce repeated decoration and minimise text. Prefer authentic field photography and evidence-led maps. Use progressive reveals where they explain a decision. Keep probability colours consistent, use distinct colours for selected sites, and do not colour collection productivity as transmission risk. Pair comparable maps with identical extents and scales. Export scientific figures as vector graphics and high-resolution raster alternatives. Use self-contained legends, units and captions.

The app segment needs an offline fallback. Do not depend on a live network or app demonstration. Speaker notes should include interpretation, provenance, transitions and timings.

## Evidence checks before building the final deck

- Verify the applicable entomological assessment guidance and the meaning of the 6,000-fly target. Earlier project documents differ on whether it applies per site or per assessment unit. Use Claudio's clarified unit as the intended narrative while resolving source wording before presenting a regulatory claim.
- Reconcile the historical storydeck, current spatial model products and adaptive sampling datasets. Their site counts and periods differ.
- Verify the current operational ranking strategy and map outputs, including state-specific restrictions.
- Select validation evidence that genuinely addresses prediction at unsampled sites. Do not substitute in-sample fit.
- Do not claim realised field improvement from predicted catches or analytical gains.
- Check current adaptive calculations against their appropriate eco-zone baseline. The handoff identifies superseded figures and extrapolation in the coupled model.
- Distinguish prospective field validation from completed work. Confirm collaborator credits and whether any precise site locations require generalisation for conference display.
